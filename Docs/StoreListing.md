## Short Description

## Full Description
Rediscover the timeless puzzle game that captivated millions, completely remastered for modern mobile play!

LINE 98: Color Lines brings the beloved classic to life with breathtaking crystal visuals, tactile physics, soothing audio, and seamless touch controls. Move colorful crystal balls across a 9×9 grid to create lines of 5 or more of the same color. Clear lines horizontally, vertically, or diagonally to keep the board open and score huge combos!

### Features:
- **Classic Endless Mode:** The authentic Line 98 experience. Challenge yourself to beat your personal high score.
- **Daily Challenge:** A unique, deterministic daily puzzle with a shared seed. Build your streak and master the board!
- **Zen Mode:** Relax with endless play, peaceful ambient sounds, no score pressure, and no distractions.
- **Modern 2.5D Presentation:** Stunning gem-like crystal spheres, recessed board cells, and crisp visual feedback.
- **Satisfying Game Feel:** Dynamic animations, custom sound effects, and rewarding multi-tier clear celebrations.
- **Full Offline Play:** Enjoy the full puzzle experience anytime, anywhere without an internet connection.
- **Multilingual Support:** Available in English and Vietnamese at launch.
